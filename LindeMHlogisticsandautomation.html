Trusted Globally. Delivered Locally.


Linde Material Handling New Zealand provides material handling, automation and intralogistics solutions to businesses throughout New Zealand.

Linde's portfolio includes forklift trucks, warehouse equipment, automation systems, fleet management technology, energy solutions and aftermarket support services. Linde products and technologies are used across industries including logistics, warehousing, manufacturing, food and beverage, retail and distribution.

As part of the global Linde Material Handling network, the New Zealand operation combines international expertise with local sales, service and technical support. In addition to equipment supply and maintenance, Linde offers consultation services relating to material flow, fleet management and warehouse operations.

A team of technical specialists is available to analyse equipment utilisation, operational workflows and material handling requirements, helping organisations make informed decisions on fleet performance, warehouse efficiency and operational planning.

Through a nationwide network, Linde supports New Zealand businesses with equipment, technology and services across a broad range of materials handling and intralogistics applications.

Innovations in Performance & Safety



Order picking with the Linde MV01: Multi-functional, Efficient AND Cost-effective


Compact and versatile, the MV01 is a task support vehicle that delivers impressive manoeuvrability to accomplish a wide variety of applications in the workplace. Thanks to its stable mast design, the platform can be raised up to three metres, bringing its total picking height to a whopping 4.6 metres.

Operators can also drive and lift at the same time, greatly increasing efficiency, and its powerful DC motor can reach speeds of up to 6.5 km/h without a load (five km/h with a load).

The MV01 is powered by a long-lasting 24-volt Lithium-ION battery. Its conveniently integrated charger allows users to recharge the battery during short breaks quickly and easily, so the order picker is always available to get the job done.



Materials handling automation is no longer science fiction - it is here and it works.


Automated procedures ensure consistent, efficient and flexible processes. Autonomous work teams execute repetitive handling processes independently and reliably.

In materials handling terms, the most consistent and repetitive tasks lend themselves readily to automation.

Automation improves your goods handling times and helps minimise personal injury as well as damage to goods and infrastructure as machines don't get bored, tired, make mistakes, or take shortcuts.

Combined with innovative navigation technology, which enables robotic vehicles to orient themselves without the need for mirrors or rails, Linde offers a reliable automation solution that helps customers optimise their internal material flows and significantly reduce costs.



The Lithium-ION Battery Revolution in Material Handling


In addition to the benefits of battery-electric equipment in general, such as reduced emissions, increased energy efficiency and avoiding the need to store dangerous fuels, Li-ION batteries bring further advantages because they can be charged quickly. This rapid charge capability, combined with higher energy density and high overall system efficiency, allows the batteries to provide up to 30% more energy than lead-acid.

Furthermore, the batteries suffer no loss of performance, even when the charge is low.



Smart safety systems: Prevent workplace accidents with Linde Safety Guard


The Linde Safety Guard system is based on transmitters positioned on industrial trucks, walls, and racking, as well as carried by staff members. Its sensors ensure rapid detection, early warning, and avoidance of hazardous situations. The system transmits warnings to pedestrians and truck operators, sending signals through walls and racking, eliminating the need for line of sight.



Forklift Safety Technology: Smarter Assistance Systems, Safer Workplaces


Linde offers a number of innovative assistance systems designed to maximise safety and improve efficiency and productivity.

Linde Safety Pilot (LSP) is designed to prevent tipping accidents for counterbalanced forklifts. LSP has a range of sensors that measure data such as lift height, tilt angle, and load weight at various locations on the truck, removing any guesswork and instantly identifying danger.



FleetFOCUS: Providing Smarter Data for Efficient & Safe Warehousing


Operating in real time, FleetFOCUS delivers key WHS compliance and cost efficiency data via an online portal on a user's PC, laptop, tablet or smartphone—anytime, anywhere.

The system monitors usage and service data, and can automatically tag out unsafe vehicles to ensure only authorised personnel can activate the unit once set.

The included GPS functionality allows fleet managers to quickly track vehicle movements, including the model, serial number and the last time it reported in.
